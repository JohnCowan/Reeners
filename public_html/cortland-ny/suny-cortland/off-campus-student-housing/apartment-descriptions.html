Apartment Descriptions

 

The Suites offers the finest luxury apartments with contemporary building, amenities and safety features for all your off-campus student housing needs.

 

Included

  • Spacious open floor plan
  • Range from 1000 to 1500 sq. ft. of new living space
  • Three private bedrooms each approx. 120 sq. ft. Plus large closet space
  • Large bathrooms with double sinks, full size tub/shower, linen closet
  • Complete kitchens with full size refrigerator, microwave, dishwasher, range, and snack-bar or dining area
  • Fully furnished including full size beds, desks, dressers, and living room
  • Radiant in-floor heating. (The most reliable and comfortable type of heating that currently exists)
  • State-of-the-art construction for Sound, Comfort, Natural Lighting, and Ventilation

 

Amenities

  • Cable and high-speed internet included
  • All utilities included
  • Trash removal included
  • Free tenant parking
  • Internal access to laundry rooms
  • Each suite has access to large shared roofed porch or deck
  • Landscaped grounds with aprox. 10,000 sq. ft. green space

 

Safety

  • Emergency Exits - well marked and lighted
  • Fire Sprinkler System throughout entire building
  • Smoke detectors in all bedrooms and common areas
  • Carbon Monoxide detector in suite common areas
  • CCTV Building Surveillance
  • Parking and Perimeter areas are always well lit for tenant safety and security
